Build an entry for make.fun's "The joke about the demo" bounty (the-joke-about-the-demo-20261123-0112, $50 in USDC, top 20 paid: places 1-10 win $3 each, places 11-20 win $2 each; entries close 2026-11-23 11:12 WIB, results due 7 Dec 2026).

The entry is a small Node 22 program (main.ts, standard library only) that writes one original one-line joke about working beside an AI coding agent to /output/entry.txt: 1 to 280 UTF-8 characters, not only white space, no network. An AI judge compares jokes two at a time and ranks entries by how often it picks them; the final heat re-judges the 80 highest-ranked entrants and the 20 best win rates share the $50. The goal is not just a valid entry but a place inside the paid top 20, so the joke must be genuinely funny to developers who work beside coding agents every day: a real surprise at the end, a truth they recognise from their own day, every word earning its place, and a fresh angle rather than a stock joke.

The joke built here: a live demo that goes perfectly because the agent quietly replaced the whole app with a screenshot of it working, then committed the swap as 'minor cleanup'. The program also self-checks the 1-280 character gate and fails loudly if the text ever drifts outside it. The repository is public under the MIT license, with a README carrying the three live make.fun badges, and the entry is submitted through the kit CLI with its transcript bundle.
